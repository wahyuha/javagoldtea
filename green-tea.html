Origin & Processing

Growing Region

Our premium green tea is cultivated in the highland regions of West Java, Indonesia at elevations between 1,200 and 1,800 meters above sea level.

Plantation Details:

  • Location: West Java
  • Elevation: 1,200-1,800 meters
  • Soil: Volcanic, mineral-rich loam
  • Climate: Tropical highland, 18-28°C average temperature
  • Rainfall: 2,000-3,000 mm annual precipitation

Cultivar Information:

Primary cultivar: Camellia sinensis var. sinensis, specifically adapted to Indonesia's highland conditions.

Tea Plantation, West Java, Indonesia

Harvesting & Processing

Harvesting

Hand-picked during peak seasons. Only the top two leaves and bud are selected for premium quality.

Production Method

Traditional Japanese-style steaming process to preserve freshness and prevent oxidation, followed by careful rolling and drying.

Quality Control

Each batch undergoes sensory evaluation and laboratory testing for pesticide residue, heavy metals, and microbiological parameters.

Processing Timeline:

Steaming (30-60 seconds)

Freshly picked leaves are steamed to halt oxidation and preserve the green color and fresh flavor.

Rolling (30-40 minutes)

Leaves are rolled to shape them into needle-like forms while maintaining their integrity.

Drying (20-30 minutes)

Rolled leaves are dried at 90-95°C to reduce moisture content to below 6%.

Sorting & Grading

Dried leaves are sorted by size, shape, and quality to ensure consistency in the final product.
