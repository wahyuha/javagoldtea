About JavaGoldTea

JavaGoldTea is a premium Indonesian tea export company, dedicated to bringing the finest teas from Indonesia's lush plantations to global markets. With decades of experience in tea cultivation and processing, we ensure exceptional quality in every batch.

Our teas are sourced from carefully selected plantations across Java, Sumatra, and other Indonesian islands, where ideal growing conditions and traditional knowledge combine to create distinctive, flavorful teas.
