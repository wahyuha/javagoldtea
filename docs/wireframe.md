# 🧩 Wireframe Structure

## Home Page
- Hero Image (Large)
- Headline + Subheadline (Selling Hook)
- Call to Action (Button)
- Short About Us
- Highlight Products
- Gallery (Photo Grid)
- Testimonials Slider
- Latest Blog Posts (3 cards preview)
- Footer (Quick Links + Contact Info)

## About Us Page
- Company Story (Text + Photos)
- Vision & Mission
- Team Members (Photo + Short Bio)
- Company Experience Timeline
- Awards Showcase
- Certifications Display

## Products Page
- Product List (Grid)
- Filter (Category buttons optional)

## Product Detail Page
- Product Image (Main + Thumbnails)
- Description Text
- Specifications Table
- Packaging Info
- Call to Action (Request a Quote)

## Blog Page
- Blog Articles Grid (Thumbnail + Title + Excerpt)
- Category Filter
- Search Bar (Optional)

## Blog Detail Page
- Title
- Featured Image
- Article Content (from Markdown)
- Related Articles
- Contact CTA

## Contact Page
- Simple Inquiry Form (Name, Email, Message)
- WhatsApp Button
- (Optional) Embedded Map
